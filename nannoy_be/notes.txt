What RecursionError: maximum recursion depth exceeded means is that Python kept calling the same function over and over until it hit its safety limit, then stopped execution instead of letting the call stack grow forever. In your traceback, the loop starts when __init__ sets self.health = 100 in game_character_stat_lab.py, which triggers the recursive setter.
